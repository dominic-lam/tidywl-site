Support

Need help?

Most issues have a quick fix. Start here before reaching out.

Common issues

  • Sync stuck or showing the wrong count? See "Why does my video count differ" in the FAQ. YouTube hides deleted, private, region-locked, copyright-blocked, expired livestream, suspended-channel, and age-restricted videos from its own API.
  • Wrong account showing? Refresh the YouTube tab to pick up your current login, then reopen the dashboard.
  • Extension not loading? Make sure you are on youtube.com/playlist?list=WL and signed in.

Still stuck? Email us

For anything the common fixes above don't cover: bug reports, questions, feedback, Ko-fi issues.
