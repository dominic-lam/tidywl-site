Why I built this

I hit the 5,000 limit. Then I lost too many evenings to YouTube.

For years I dumped everything into Watch Later like it was a bottomless pit. Then videos I thought I'd saved stopped showing up: YouTube stops at 5,000, with no warning, and its only fix is removing them one by one. So I built TidyWL, the dashboard I wanted: see which channels fill your list, find anything in it, and clear hundreds at once.

Dominic, indie developer

Privacy

Your videos stay yours

No accounts. No server holding your playlist. TidyWL works in your browser with the YouTube session you already have, and your playlist stays on your computer.

Three things do leave. To publish the totals at the top of this page, the extension sends tidywl.com usage counts, such as videos deleted, likes removed and videos moved, with a random install ID. Never which videos, never your playlist, never your Google account. It is always on in Chrome, where uninstalling stops it, and Firefox has a toggle for it. If you subscribe to Pro, your licence key goes to tidywl.com to check that the subscription is live. And Send to Gemini Notebook, which you start yourself, hands the links you picked to Google under your own login. Every detail is in the privacy policy.
