Why I built this

I hit the 5,000 limit. Then I lost too many evenings to YouTube.

For years I dumped everything into Watch Later like it was a bottomless pit. Turns out it isn't. YouTube has a hidden 5,000-video limit, and once you hit it, new videos silently fail to save: no error, no warning. The only way I noticed was when videos I thought I'd saved weren't there anymore.

The "fix" was even worse: no bulk delete, no filtering, no search. Just me, scrolling through an endless list, removing videos one by one. Every 1-2 months I'd lose an evening to it.

I finally snapped and built TidyWL, a free Chrome extension that gives you an actual dashboard for your Watch Later. See a breakdown by channel, filter and sort by date or duration, search by title, and bulk-delete hundreds of videos in seconds.

Dominic, indie developer

Features

Everything you wish YouTube had

Beat the 5,000 Limit

YouTube silently stops saving videos past 5,000. TidyWL helps you clean out old videos so you can keep adding new ones.

Unique to TidyWL

Delete Hundreds at Once

YouTube forces you to delete videos one by one. TidyWL lets you select hundreds with checkboxes and bulk-delete in seconds.

Download Transcripts in Bulk

Select any number of videos and get one timestamped text file each, named after the video. TidyWL checks which ones actually have captions first, then tells you exactly what it couldn't get and why. Runs in a small visible window, about three seconds per video.

Send to Gemini Notebook

Push your chosen videos straight into a Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) as sources — a new notebook or one you already have — without leaving the dashboard. It's the one feature that leaves your machine: the links you pick go to Google under your own login. Nothing else does.

Channel Breakdown

See your Watch Later organized by channel. Find all the videos from that one YouTuber you binged three months ago in one click.

By Topic tab

Automatically groups your Watch Later by recurring keywords in titles (e.g. "recipe", "react", "interview"). Click a topic to filter down to it, or hide it to get it out of the way — as many topics as you like. Runs 100% client-side, no API calls, no data sent anywhere.

How it works

Step 2

Step 3

Browse, filter, and bulk-delete. Or reorder and export if you'd rather tidy than purge.

YouTube caps every playlist, including Watch Later, at 5000 items. Once you hit the limit, adding more fails silently. The only fix is to delete older ones. This extension makes that possible without losing your afternoon.
Install the extension, go to your Watch Later page, click "Open Dashboard", hit Sync, then select videos (individually, by channel, by filter, or with Cmd/Ctrl+A) and press Delete. YouTube's API requires sequential deletion, so large batches take a few seconds.
Select the videos you want and click Download Transcripts. You get one timestamped text file per video, named after the video title. TidyWL first asks YouTube which of them have captions at all, then opens one small visible window and works through the rest, roughly three seconds per video, and closes it when it is done. Leave that window on top while it runs: YouTube only builds transcripts in a window it considers visible. Not every video has captions, so the summary at the end lists exactly which ones were skipped and why.

This is the one feature that sends anything off your machine: the links you picked go to Google under your own Google login. Free Gemini Notebook accounts cap a notebook at 50 sources and batches larger than that have not been tested, so keep them under 50 for now. Videos Gemini Notebook cannot ingest, such as ones with no captions, private ones, or ones uploaded in the last few days, come back reported as skipped.

Support

Need help?

Most issues have a quick fix. Start here before reaching out.

Common issues

  • Sync stuck or showing the wrong count? Check the hidden-video explainer in the FAQ above. YouTube hides deleted, private, region-locked, copyright-blocked, expired livestream, suspended-channel, and age-restricted videos from its own API.
  • Wrong account showing? Refresh the YouTube tab to pick up your current login, then reopen the dashboard.
  • Extension not loading? Make sure you are on youtube.com/playlist?list=WL and signed in.
