Questions about your key

Is my key emailed?
No. Copy it somewhere safe or bookmark this page.
How many computers can use it?
Up to three per key. Deactivate a device from the same panel in the About card to free a seat.
I lost my key. How do I get it back?

Every Paddle receipt, the first and each renewal, shows a Transaction ID that starts with txn_. Paste it into Find my key on this page, or into Have a key? in the TidyWL About card, which finds the key and switches Pro on in one step. On a computer where Pro is already on, the About card shows the key too.

Anyone holding a Transaction ID can see the key, so keep your receipts to yourself.

Still stuck?
Email support@tidywl.com from the address you paid with.
