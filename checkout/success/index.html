Fetching your key…

Activate it

  1. Open YouTube and open the TidyWL dashboard.
  2. Click the ⓘ About button in the top right.
  3. Under TidyWL Pro, click Have a key?, paste the key and click Activate. The badge turns PRO.

One key works on up to three computers. Deactivate a device from the same panel to free a seat.

Keep this page

Bookmark it or copy the key somewhere safe: it comes back on this page as long as the link keeps its _ptxn or txn part.

Lost your key?

Every Paddle receipt, the first and each renewal, shows a Transaction ID that starts with txn_. Paste it into Find my key on this page, or into Have a key? in the TidyWL About card, which finds the key and switches Pro on in one step. On a computer where Pro is already on, the About card shows the key too.

Anyone holding a Transaction ID can see the key, so keep your receipts to yourself. Still stuck? Email support@tidywl.com from the address you paid with.
