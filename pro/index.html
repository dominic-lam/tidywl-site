What Pro adds

Eight things Pro gives you that free does not

Rules

Run when you open YouTube. They never delete a video you could still watch: they move it to an archive playlist you own.

Finished videos, archived

Anything you watched past 95% moves to the archive. Near-zero regret, so it runs first and earns your trust for the rest.

Dead entries, removed

Private, deleted and unavailable videos are cleared out. They cannot be moved, so this one deletes, after a preview.

Bottom 10%, every month

Once a month, the oldest tenth of your Watch Later moves to the archive. Set the percentage yourself. The dashboard tells you the size your list settles at.

Pin

Mark a video "keep forever". Every rule skips it. The one you saved for a trip stays saved.

Safety

For the bulk actions you already have.

Preview before you act

Every bulk delete, move, unlike or rule shows the exact list first. Untick anything, then go.

Undo, for 90 days

What you removed is kept on this device for 90 days. Restore one video or a whole batch, back into the playlist it came from.

Power

Get back to a view in one click, and be heard first.

Saved views

Name a filter combination, say unwatched, over 20 minutes, from three channels, and open it in one click.

Requests and support, answered first

Your feature requests and bug reports are read first and weigh most in what gets built next. Support emails from subscribers go to the front of the queue.
