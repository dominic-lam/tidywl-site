How-to

How to get Netflix's old My List page back

Netflix now shows My List as one row you scroll sideways. Here is how to get the whole page back, in Netflix's own menu.

By Dominic Lam, the developer of TidyWL for Netflix·{{ page.date | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}·Updated {{ page.last_modified_at | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}

The short answer: Netflix has no setting to switch back, but on a computer the free Chrome extension TidyWL for Netflix (listed on the Chrome Web Store as Watch Later Tidy for Netflix) puts the page back. It adds a My List link beside My Netflix in Netflix's top menu. Click it, and every title on your My List appears as a grid of cards, in Netflix's order, the way the page looked before. There is nothing to set up.

What changed

Around September 15, 2026, Netflix's website replaced the My List page with My Netflix. My List is now one row on that page, between Continue Watching and Trailers You've Watched, and you scroll it sideways a few titles at a time with the arrows at its ends. People who kept long lists lost the one screen where they could see everything they had saved, and spot what was about to leave Netflix. More on that in What happened to Netflix's My List page.

Get the My List page back, in three steps

  1. Add the extension. Add Watch Later Tidy for Netflix to Chrome. It is free and there is no account to make.
  2. Open Netflix. Go to netflix.com in Chrome and choose your profile.
  3. Click My List. It is in Netflix's top menu, next to My Netflix. Your whole list opens as a page of cards, in Netflix's order.

What the page does

  • Every title at once. The whole list, scrolled down rather than sideways, in the order Netflix keeps it.
  • Netflix's own preview. Rest the pointer on a card and Netflix's preview opens: play it, rate it, or take it off your list. Click a card to open the title.
  • IMDb ratings in the corner of every card.
  • Always current. It reads your list from Netflix each time you open it, so there is nothing to sync. A title you remove leaves the page at once, and Netflix's Undo brings it back.
  • In your language. The link follows your profile's language in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese and Chinese, and is in English otherwise.

Want to sort or filter it? Open the dashboard

The page keeps Netflix's order. To change the order or narrow it down, click Open in Watch Later Tidy at the top of the page, or the extension's button in Chrome's toolbar. A dashboard opens over Netflix with every title as a card. Press Sync once and it remembers your list, so it opens instantly after that.

  • See what is leaving. Sync marks every title Netflix is about to remove, with Netflix's own last-day sentence, and one filter shows only those. The My List page labels them too.
  • Filter by genre, type, IMDb rating, runtime, and whether you have already watched it, for films and for shows.
  • Sort by IMDb rating, runtime, year or title, instead of the order Netflix chose.
  • Clear it out: tick as many titles as you like, review the exact list, and remove them in one go.
  • Export the whole list, or what you filtered, as CSV or JSON.

All of that is free. Everything TidyWL for Netflix does.

Good to know

  • It runs in Chrome on a computer. No extension can run in Chrome for Android or in Netflix's TV apps.
  • Looking at your list changes nothing on Netflix. A title leaves My List only if you remove it yourself, and then it leaves on every device, because it is the same list.
  • The My List page stores nothing it reads. The privacy policy lists every request the extension makes.
  • TidyWL for Netflix is an independent extension, not made by or affiliated with Netflix.
