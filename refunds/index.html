Refund policy

Seven days, no questions. Here is the rest.

Applies to every TidyWL Pro purchase made through Paddle, our merchant of record. Published 2026-09-02. Seller: Tsz Hin Lam (Dominic Lam), operating as TidyWL, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

The short version

  • Any charge, within 7 days: full refund, no questions. That includes renewals. If a yearly plan renewed and you meant to cancel, tell us within 7 days of the charge and it comes back.
  • After 7 days: cancel any time, no partial refund of the period you are in. Cancelling stops the next charge and Pro runs to the end of what you paid for. One exception below, for when YouTube breaks a feature.
  • Paddle returns the money to the card or account you paid with. Most refunds show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

How to ask

Email support@tidywl.com from the address you used at checkout, or reply to your Paddle receipt. Include the order number if you have it; if not, the email address is enough. We aim to answer within two business days, and we do not ask why.

You can also go straight to Paddle at paddle.net, which is the buyer support site for every purchase Paddle processes. Either route reaches the same refund.

What happens after a refund

  • Your licence key is deactivated and the extension goes back to the free version at the next check, usually within a day.
  • Nothing in your browser is deleted. Your playlist caches, your settings and anything you pinned or saved stay where they are.
  • You can buy Pro again later. A refunded key cannot be reactivated; a new purchase issues a new one.

If YouTube breaks a Pro feature

TidyWL works with YouTube's web endpoints rather than an official API, and YouTube can change them at any time. If a Pro feature stops working because of a YouTube change and we have not restored it within 30 days of it stopping, you can ask for a refund of the unused part of a yearly subscription, counted from the day it stopped. On a monthly plan, cancel and the next charge does not happen. If a change cannot be fixed at all, we say so on this site and in the extension, stop selling Pro, and refund the remaining term to every yearly subscriber without being asked.

Chargebacks

Please ask us first. A refund is one email and takes days; a chargeback takes weeks, costs a dispute fee on our side, and ends with the same result. If you have already filed one, tell us and we will confirm it to Paddle rather than contest it.
