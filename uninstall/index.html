Reinstall: Chrome · Firefox · Edge

YouTube changes things. Most fixes go out within hours.

TidyWL works with YouTube's web endpoints rather than an official API, and YouTube changes them without notice. Most fixes go out within hours, with no extension update to install. Bigger changes need a new version.

So it may already be working again. The Discord is the quickest place to see whether it's a known problem.

If YouTube stopped playing videos on your account, or told you to try again later, that's a temporary limit on how many requests one account can make in a short time. In my experience it normally clears by itself in 30 to 60 minutes, so give it that long before you try anything else. Recent versions pace removals of thousands of videos to avoid it. This can be a temporary pause on YouTube's side.
